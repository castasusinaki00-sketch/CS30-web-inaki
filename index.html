    KEY TERMS

    Writing Diagnostics Glossary

    Understanding the metrics that define whether an essay retains personal authenticity or slips into automated dependency.

    📊 Lexical Variety

    The proportion of unique words to total word count (Type-Token Ratio). High diversity signals rich personal vocabulary, while low scores indicate repetitive phrasing.

    🧠 Cognitive Offloading

    The practice of relying on automated tools to carry mental labor (outlining, ideation, syntax) rather than exercising internal critical reasoning.

    ⚖️ The Confidence Gap

    The psychological disparity where a writer scores well with AI assistance but experiences acute anxiety when tasked with composing an essay unassisted.

    🔍 Syntactic Density

    The ratio of clauses and complex sentence constructions. Varied syntactic density creates organic rhythm; uniform density sounds mechanical.

    COMMUNITY FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Practical advice for students, creators, and professionals balancing writing rigor with generative AI.

    We recommend drafting a 3-point bullet outline manually first. Once you know your personal conviction, consult AI to identify counterarguments or blind spots you might have overlooked. If you generate the outline first, you unconsciously adopt the model's perspective rather than discovering your own.

    A simple test: Sit down with a blank sheet of paper and a pen, set a 10-minute timer, and write on your topic. If you experience paralysis or can't initiate sentences without an autocomplete prompt, you are likely experiencing cognitive offloading. Regular timed freewrites quickly rebuild this mental stamina.

    Never accept full paragraph replacements. Ask your AI assistant for specific grammar flags, passive voice callouts, or alternative transition words. Choose and type the corrections manually so the rhythm remains natural to your speaking cadence.

    WriteCraft analyzes the ratio of distinct lemmas against the total word count, evaluates average sentence length, and flags syntactic repetition. It highlights whether your piece reflects organic human variation or the balanced neutrality characteristic of automated text.
